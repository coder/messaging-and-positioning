# Caring Provider

## Who They Are

The Caring Provider is a technical practitioner motivated by delivering a great experience to the people who depend on them. This is a mindset, not a job title: it most often shows up in platform engineers and other technical practitioners, but it describes a motivation rather than a fixed role. A Caring Provider can exist alongside other personas within the same organization, and even within the same person, depending on the situation.

## Core Motivation

Caring Providers want to give the builders and teams who rely on them a great, productive experience, because they see that experience as directly tied to the value their organization and its mission can deliver.

## What They Care About

- Giving developers and other internal users a smooth, productive day-to-day experience.
- Operational quality: tools and platforms that hold up in practice, not just in a demo.
- Reducing toil and friction for the people they support.
- Being able to experiment, adopt, and prove value on their own initiative before asking for organizational buy-in.

## What They're Skeptical Of

- Tools that look impressive in a pitch but create more operational burden once adopted.
- Rigid, prescriptive platforms that reduce their control over how they support their users.
- Vendors or advocates who don't understand the practical, day-to-day realities of running infrastructure.

## How to Engage Them

Lead with technical credibility and practitioner-to-practitioner conversation, not a top-down sales pitch. Meet Caring Providers where they already experiment and evaluate tools, and let them validate value hands-on before introducing a broader organizational conversation. See the [company message house](../../company/message-house.md) for how Coder frames its technical narrative.

## Where They Show Up

Caring Providers most often appear as:

- [Platform Engineer](../platform-engineer.md)
- [Developer](../developer.md)

The same title doesn't guarantee the same persona. A platform engineer can act as a Caring Provider in one organization, while a technical leader with a different title takes on that motivation elsewhere.

## Related Personas

Caring Providers are usually the entry point for adoption but don't always hold budget. A durable business case usually requires connecting a Caring Provider's technical validation to an [Opportunist](./opportunist.md) or a [Protectionist](./protectionist.md) who can advocate for the investment in business terms.
