# Opportunist

## Who They Are

The Opportunist is a stakeholder motivated by cost and efficiency. This is a mindset, not a job title: it can appear in an executive, a finance-oriented technical leader, or anyone else who evaluates AI development investment primarily through the lens of return on investment.

## Core Motivation

Opportunists see AI development as an opportunity if it can be made efficient and cost-effective, and as a liability if it's inefficient or expensive. Their central question is whether an investment produces a clear, defensible return.

## What They Care About

- Measurable return on investment and productivity gains.
- Avoiding wasted spend on tools or infrastructure that don't produce a clear efficiency gain.
- Flexibility to adjust course if a cost assumption turns out to be wrong.
- Being able to build a business case that holds up to scrutiny.

## What They're Skeptical Of

- Investments with unclear or unproven return on investment.
- Vendor lock-in that limits their ability to change course later if costs don't materialize as expected.
- Claims of efficiency or savings that aren't backed by concrete evidence.

## How to Engage Them

Lead with a quantifiable value story: productivity gained, time saved, or cost avoided, backed by evidence rather than assertion. Emphasize flexibility and choice, since Opportunists are wary of commitments that reduce their ability to adjust later. See the [company message house](../../company/message-house.md) and the [Compute/Resource Optimization](../../use-cases/compute-resource-optimization.md) use case for quantifiable value narratives.

## Where They Show Up

Opportunists can appear in a range of roles, including:

- [CTO](../cto.md)
- Technical or engineering leaders evaluating cost and efficiency of AI-assisted development

The same title doesn't guarantee the same persona. A leader who is an Opportunist at one organization may act as a [Protectionist](./protectionist.md) at another, with someone else in the organization taking on the opposite motivation.

## Related Personas

Opportunists and [Protectionists](./protectionist.md) most often hold budget and final decision authority, while [Caring Providers](./caring-provider.md) generate the initial technical validation. A durable business case typically needs to speak to whichever of these two motivations is present, not just the technical one.
