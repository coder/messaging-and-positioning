# Market Landscape

This directory holds battlecards for the vendors and product categories that come up alongside Coder in customer conversations. Each battlecard covers one vendor or category and explains how it relates to Coder, where it fits, where it stops, and how to position Coder alongside it.

Start with [Why Coder](../company/why-coder.md) for the broader view. Coder rarely faces direct, feature-for-feature competition. Most alternatives solve an adjacent problem, are legacy development environment products in decline, or amount to building the platform in-house. Battlecards apply that view to a specific vendor or category rather than restating it.

## Guidelines

- **Keep it public.** Everything in this repository is public. Leave out pricing intelligence, win/loss details, named-customer deals, and any other non-public information.
- **Link rather than duplicate.** Product message houses and FAQs already describe where specific vendors fit relative to each Coder product. Link to those sections instead of restating them, so an update in one place doesn't leave the other out of date.
- **Use established terminology.** Refer to large AI companies as AI providers. Describe tools that complement Coder as complementary rather than as competitors.
- **Stay factual.** Only make claims about other vendors that their public materials support, and keep those claims current.
